## Matteo Raciti

I spend about six months a year at sea as the HVAC-R Engineer at **Carnival Cruise Line**, running a ship's entire HVAC & Refrigeration plant somewhere between the US, the Caribbean and Australia.

Life at sea means months offline, so I build what I can maintain, or what can maintain itself: self-hosted infrastructure, home automation, and native apps. This profile is a curated slice of that work.

- **Homelab** on Proxmox: clustered nodes, containers, off-site backups, and remote access from wherever the ship docks.
- **Smart home** with Home Assistant and Homey, plus cameras, sensors and automations that run without me.
- **AI agents** that do real ops work: they watch the lab, handle updates and report back while I'm mid-ocean.
- **Code** mostly in Swift, Python and Rust, whenever something I need doesn't exist yet.
